About & review policy

How we research product claims and disclose commercial relationships.

Our purpose

Men's Supplement Guide publishes educational buyer's guides designed to help readers understand supplement labels, marketing claims, research limitations, safety considerations and purchase terms.

How reviews are prepared

We compare the manufacturer's current public information with government health guidance and published research. We clearly distinguish research on individual ingredients from research on a finished product. We do not claim personal use, laboratory testing or medical expertise unless those activities genuinely occurred and can be documented.

Affiliate disclosure

Some outbound product links may be affiliate links. If a reader makes a qualifying purchase, the publisher may earn a commission at no additional cost to the reader. Compensation does not justify inventing results, testimonials or medical claims.

Corrections and updates

Supplement labels, prices, guarantees and research can change. Each review displays a checked or updated date. Material errors should be corrected promptly once verified.

Editorial limitations

The Testosil review was based on publicly available information and was not a hands-on product test. It should not be used as medical advice or as a substitute for diagnosis.
